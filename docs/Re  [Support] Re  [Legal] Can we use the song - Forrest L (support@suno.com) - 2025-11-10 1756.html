Hi Jon,

No worries! Yes, you can subscribe with the monthly plan for however long you'd like!

By default, the subscription will automatically renew at the end of the billing period, however, you can cancel at any time by visiting https://suno.com/account then choosing Cancel in the plan options. Here's a quick look at where to cancel if you're subscribed to a Pro plan:


Cancel is located under the Free plan, as you will be downgraded from Pro to Free at the end of the billing cycle!

*Note: Subscriptions that were created via Google Play or the App Store must be canceled through the applicable store and cannot be modified on suno.com!

If you experience any issues or have any additional questions, please don't hesitate to reach back out!
